Third-party material in this repository

The MIT license in LICENSE covers the code. It does not cover the dataset
material below, which keeps its own terms. No dataset images are included;
code/download.py fetches them, and the images sent to the models are rebuilt
by code/render.py. The model answers in data/calls were produced by the models
listed in code/items.py.

V* Bench (Wu and Xie, CVPR 2024)
  License: MIT for the code and annotations, https://github.com/penghao-wu/vstar;
  the images come from SA-1B and keep the SA-1B research license.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/craigwu/vstar_bench at revision
  d9ae62c903da0c98336e85c5ee89cd863b04b4da
  Included: the questions, options and target boxes of the 191
  direct_attributes and relative_position items in data/items/photos.jsonl.
  Changes: options shuffled with a fixed seed; a "cannot tell" option added.
  Cite: Wu and Xie, V*: Guided Visual Search as a Core Mechanism in
  Multimodal LLMs, CVPR 2024.

HR-Bench (Wang et al., AAAI 2025)
  License: No license stated; used only for evaluation; images not
  redistributed.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench at revision
  83b9013d6293b85dc507e87199ca52517536939c (hr_bench_8k.parquet)
  Included: 150 questions with their options and answers in
  data/items/photos.jsonl. Changes: options shuffled with a fixed seed; a
  "cannot tell" option added.
  Cite: Wang et al., Divide, Conquer and Combine: A Training-Free Framework
  for High-Resolution Image Perception in Multimodal Large Language Models,
  AAAI 2025.

MME-RealWorld-Lite (Zhang et al., ICLR 2025)
  License: Apache-2.0 for the benchmark, https://github.com/yfzhang114/MME-RealWorld;
  the images keep the terms of their source datasets.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/yifanzhang114/MME-RealWorld-Lite at
  revision 0e378f64aea87bb2c30dae8bc600f99fae966711
  Included: 130 Remote Sensing and 130 Monitoring perception questions with
  their options and answers in data/items/photos.jsonl. Changes: a "cannot
  tell" option added.
  Cite: Zhang et al., MME-RealWorld: Could Your Multimodal LLM Challenge
  High-Resolution Real-World Scenarios that are Difficult for Humans?,
  ICLR 2025.
